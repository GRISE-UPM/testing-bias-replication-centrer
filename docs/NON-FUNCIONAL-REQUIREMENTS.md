# Non-functional requirements

- <a id="nfr-authentication"></a>The system shall require researchers (see [c-researcher](https://github.com/GRISE-UPM/testing-bias-replication-centrer/edit/main/docs/VISION.md#c-researcher)) to register using an external identity provider, such as Google. After registration, the interested researchers (see [c-interested-researcher](https://github.com/GRISE-UPM/testing-bias-replication-centrer/edit/main/docs/VISION.md#c-interested-researcher)) shall receive a confirmation email and a drop-out link.

- <a id="nfr-participation"></a>Request for participation shall be validated by an organizer.

- <a id="nfr-logging"></a>The access to replication materials (see [c-replication-material](https://github.com/GRISE-UPM/testing-bias-replication-centrer/edit/main/docs/VISION.md#c-replication-material)) and results (see [c-replication-result](https://github.com/GRISE-UPM/testing-bias-replication-centrer/edit/main/docs/VISION.md#c-replication-result)) shall be logged.
